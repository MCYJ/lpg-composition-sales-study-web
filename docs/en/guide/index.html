Study guide

01 / CONCEPT

Choose the fraction basis

Mole fraction divides constituent mol by total mol; mass fraction divides constituent mass by total mass. Choose the basis before applying the input. Switching the basis reinterprets the same number rather than automatically converting the case.

02 / CONCEPT

Distinguish the two components

The model contains C3H8 and C4H10 only. The balance of the propane fraction is butane. It neither measures a real product nor determines liquid and gas compositions. Other constituents and phase equilibrium are outside its scope.

03 / CONCEPT

Declare the teaching masses

Fixed teaching constants44g/mol and58g/mol give x=(w/44)/(w/44+(1-w)/58) from mass fraction w. These are declared educational approximations rather than precise measured material properties.

04 / CONCEPT

Read the equation coefficients

Assume C3H8+5O2→3CO2+4H2O and2C4H10+13O2→8CO2+10H2O. Multiply each constituent amount by its coefficient and add. Theoretical equation quantities are not burner or air-supply settings and do not predict actual complete combustion.

05 / CONCEPT

Check the atom ledger

Compare before-and-after C, H and O atom amounts. One mol H2O refers to molecules and corresponds to2mol H atoms and1mol O atoms. The water amount must not be directly read as gas volume or condensed-water volume.

06 / CONCEPT

Read a2mol equal-mole example

The authored case has1mol of each component. It gives11.5mol O2,7mol CO2 and9mol H2O. Teaching masses44g and58g make propane mass fraction44/102, about43.14%, rather than50%.

07 / CONCEPT

Compare the equal-mass example

A separate1mol case uses50mass% propane. Its mole fraction is29/51, about56.86%, and O2 per mixture mol is about5.647mol, compared with5.750mol at50mole%. This is not a rating of real fuels.

08 / CONCEPT

Compare on a1mol basis

When A and B have different sample sizes, total O2 alone mixes size and composition effects. Inspect total and normalized values separately. The sign of B-A is a quantity difference, not a performance, safety or suitability judgment.

09 / CONCEPT

Keep active and saved cases independent

Applying conditions saves the active case. A and B update only through their own save actions. Unapplied edits do not change diagram results. Loading restores saved conditions as active. Device-local records have no account synchronization or operator recovery.

10 / CONCEPT

Identify the model limits

The notebook excludes phase equilibrium, cylinders, air settings, excess air, flow, heat, CO/emissions, ventilation, installation, legal compliance and official scoring. It does not promise complete exam coverage or passing. Check official notices for exam information.
