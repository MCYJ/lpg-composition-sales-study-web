How to study

  1. Choose the composition basis. Changing the basis reinterprets the number rather than automatically converting the case.
  2. Enter the fraction and total mol, then apply to save the active case. Unapplied edits do not change the result diagram.
  3. Read totals separately from per1mol values. Use the atom ledger to check the equation.
  4. Update A and B with their separate save actions. Loading one case preserves the other saved case.

Study records, bookmarks, settings and A/B cases use local device storage. Check required records before removing the app or changing devices. Optional local notifications use OS permission and scheduling; question text is private by default.
